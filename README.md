# Amazon Sales & Profitability Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing and visualizing Amazon sales data to understand sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, and regional performance.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to transform raw Excel data into meaningful business insights through KPIs, DAX measures, data modeling, and interactive visualizations.

The dashboard was developed with AI assistance using Claude AI connected to Power BI through the Power BI Modeling MCP server. This AI-assisted workflow was used to create and organize Power BI visuals, develop DAX-based calculations, and support dashboard development.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

**Analyze and visualize key aspects of Amazon's sales data, including sales performance by product category, customer segment, region, state, and shipping mode. Use data analysis and visualization to identify trends, patterns, and insights that can be used to optimize sales strategies and improve profitability.**

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall sales and profitability performance.
- Compare sales performance across product categories.
- Analyze sales performance across different customer segments.
- Compare sales and profit across regions.
- Analyze state-level sales performance.
- Identify monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Compare category performance across customer segments.
- Develop interactive KPIs for business performance monitoring.
- Present the analysis through an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Transform raw data into a clear and business-focused visual report.

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### Sales by Product Category

Analyzes sales performance across major product categories such as:

- Technology
- Furniture
- Office Supplies

This helps compare the contribution of different product categories to overall sales.

### Sales Trend Analysis

A monthly and yearly sales trend visualization is used to identify changes in sales performance over time and observe periods of higher and lower sales activity.

### Regional Sales & Profitability

Sales and profit are compared across different regions to understand regional contribution and profitability.

### State-Level Sales Analysis

The dashboard provides a state-level view of sales performance, allowing users to identify states contributing significantly to overall sales.

### Category & Customer Segment Analysis

Sales are analyzed across customer segments such as:

- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

This provides a view of how different customer segments contribute to sales across product categories.
